Clariva — Sponsor Dashboard & Cohort Detail Specification
Scope
Implement/rework only:
1. Sponsor Dashboard
2. Sponsor Cohort Detail
Do not redesign the Leader Detail page yet.
The objective is to make Sponsor reporting simple, consistent, and driven by the programme cadence already configured for each programme.
Each programme already defines when required activities should happen, including for example:
- Modules
- Quizzes
- Assignments
- Triad practice
- Peer coaching
- 1:1 coaching sessions
- Mentor coaching
- Assessments
- Other required programme activities
All calculations must use this configured cadence rather than arbitrary percentages.
A. SPONSOR DASHBOARD
Purpose
The Sponsor Dashboard should answer:
How many leaders are currently in our programmes?
How many cohorts are running?
Are participants generally keeping up with their programme cadence?
Which cohort should I open for more detail?

The Dashboard must remain executive-level.
Do not show detailed coaching, goals, actions, satisfaction, individual overdue activities, or programme completion percentages here.
A1. Top KPI cards
Show exactly these four primary KPIs.
KPI	Definition	Calculation
Total Leaders	Unique leaders enrolled in currently running Sponsor-visible cohorts	Count distinct leaders with valid enrollment in a current cohort. Exclude withdrawn/cancelled enrollment if these statuses exist.
Active Leaders	Leaders whose enrollment is currently Active	Count enrollment_status = active within current cohorts
Current Cohorts	Cohorts currently being delivered	cohort_start_date <= today <= cohort_end_date, subject to actual cohort status rules
On Track	Active leaders currently keeping up with their programme cadence	Active leader has no required cadence item whose allowed deadline has passed and remains incomplete


Dashboard example
Total Leaders       Active Leaders       Current Cohorts       On Track
30                  27                   3                     24 / 27
If useful, On Track can additionally show:
89%
But the primary display should be:
24 / 27
because the Sponsor can understand the population.
A2. Definition of On Track
This is a critical business rule.
A leader is:
ON TRACK
when there is currently no required programme cadence item overdue and incomplete.
NOT ON TRACK
when at least one required cadence item has passed its allowed deadline/window and remains incomplete.
Examples:
Quiz 1 due 10 Sep
Completed 9 Sep
→ ON TRACK
Quiz 1 due 10 Sep
Today 13 Sep
Quiz incomplete
→ NOT ON TRACK
Quiz due 10 Sep
Completed late on 12 Sep
Today 13 Sep
No other overdue requirement
→ ON TRACK again
Historical lateness may still be stored for reporting, but it must not permanently make the leader Not On Track.
Before anything is due
If the cohort has started but the first cadence deadline has not yet occurred:
the active leader is currently On Track, because there is no overdue obligation.

Do not create a visible Dashboard status called:
- Not Assessable
- Not Yet Assessable
- Not Started
for this purpose.

Purpose
The Cohort Detail page should answer:
Where is this cohort in the programme?
Are participants following the programme cadence?
Which parts of the programme have been completed?
Which leaders are keeping up?
How are goals and participant experience progressing?

B1. Cohort header
Show:

Current Programme Week	Week 4 of 12
Total Leaders	20

Week 4 of 12 is sufficient and easier to understand.
B2. Main Cohort KPIs
Show:
KPI	Example
On Track	17 / 20 · 85%
Cadence Completion to Date	91%
Leaders with Goals	15 / 20
Average Goal Progress	58%
Satisfaction	Only when correct source is confirmed


B3. Cohort On Track
Same rule used everywhere.
For every active leader:
Does this leader currently have ≥1 overdue required cadence item?
If:
NO → On Track
YES → Not On Track
At Cohort level:
On Track active leaders
───────────────────────
Total active leaders
Example:
17 / 20 = 85%
Do not add another main KPI called Not On Track.
The remainder is obvious.
B4. Cadence Completion to Date
This is different from On Track.
On Track answers:
How many leaders currently have no overdue obligation?

It is a binary leader status.
Cadence Completion to Date answers:
Of what participants should have completed by today, how much have they actually completed?

It shows the depth of completion, not just whether someone has missed something.
Calculation
First calculate each leader separately.
For Leader A:
9 completed required activities due by today
10 required activities due by today

= 90%
Leader B:
10 / 10 = 100%
Leader C:
7 / 10 = 70%
Then calculate the cohort:
Average of individual leader cadence-completion percentages
Do not simply add all completed activity instances across the whole cohort and divide by everything.
Each leader should receive equal weight.
Example
Twenty leaders have:
100%
100%
90%
90%
...
Their average equals:
91%
Display:
Cadence Completion to Date — 91%

Zero denominator
If a leader has no cadence item due yet:
cadence_completion_to_date = NULL
Do not produce:
100%
For the cohort, average only valid leader percentages where at least one activity is due.
If no participant has anything due yet:
No programme activities due yet

B5. Do NOT show separate Overdue Requirement KPI
Do not create a cohort KPI like:
7 overdue activities
This can be misleading because seven overdue items could belong to one person or seven people.
The Cohort On Track metric tells the Sponsor whether leaders are keeping up.
Exact overdue requirements belong in Leader Detail.

D. GOALS
D1. Leaders with Goals
Display:
15 / 20 leaders
Calculation:
leaders with ≥1 valid Sponsor-visible goal
──────────────────────────────────────────
eligible cohort leaders
Do not combine people and goal counts into:
4 / 10
Those are different units.
D2. Average Goal Progress
For each leader with goals:
1. Calculate the average progress of that leader's valid Sponsor-visible goals.
2. Then average the resulting leader-level percentages.
Example:
Leader A = 40%
Leader B = 60%
Leader C = 80%

Cohort average = 60%
Do not treat a leader without a goal as 0%.
The separate Leaders with Goals metric already shows goal adoption.

H. PAUSED LEADERS
Enrollment lifecycle and cadence health must remain separate.
Possible enrollment statuses:
Active
Paused
Completed
Withdrawn
On Track is not an enrollment status.
For a paused leader:
Enrollment Status = Paused
On Track = —
Paused leaders should normally be excluded from the current On Track denominator while the pause is effective.
Do not classify them automatically as Not On Track.
I. DATA CONSISTENCY RULES
These rules must apply everywhere.
Rule 1
NULL ≠ 0%
If something cannot yet be calculated, return/display null.
Rule 2
0 / 0 must NEVER = 100%
Rule 3
Future cadence items:
Upcoming
not:
0% complete
Rule 4
Do not mix different units into one ratio.
Bad:
4 leaders / 10 goals
Good:
Leaders with goals: 4 / 8
Goals created: 10
Rule 5
Dashboard, Cohort and later Leader pages must use the same canonical cadence calculations.
Do not calculate the same metric differently on different screens.
J. PRIVACY
Preserve the existing Sponsor privacy model.
Sponsor must not gain access to:
- coaching notes
- session transcripts
- private chat
- private reflections
- confidential goal wording unless explicitly Sponsor-visible
- coach private observations
- coach identity where the existing privacy model withholds it
Do not weaken RLS, cohort suppression, or sponsor access controls.
