---
name: Sponsor lifecycle semantics
description: How sponsor cohort lifecycle labels should be derived when cohort pace is mixed.
---

Sponsor-facing cohort lifecycle labels must be derived from the cohort start and end dates. Aggregate pace is a separate signal and can remain mixed inside a completed cohort.

**Why:** A finished cohort can contain leaders with different pace states; using aggregate pace for the lifecycle label makes a completed programme appear active or on track.

**How to apply:** Use date-derived upcoming, active, or complete labels on sponsor cohort cards and detail pages. Reserve pace and requirement counts for progress and attention signals.
