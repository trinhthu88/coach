# Business-rule cross-check: Postgres RLS vs. frontend mirrors

This table exists because the same business rules — profile status gating, role
gating, coachee/coach allowlists, session limits, and module access — are
enforced in two places that don't share code: Postgres migrations
(`supabase/migrations/`) and the React frontend. Nothing currently keeps them
in sync automatically; this is a manual snapshot, last updated 2026-09-03 for
closing action items #2, #3, #6, and §1's `must_change_password` gap (see
"Summary of action items" at the bottom for what changed and why), plus
2026-08-30's updates for mentoring's programme-defined session limits (§3
Relationship 4, §4, §6), coachee-to-coachee peer practice (§3 Relationship 5),
the enrollment-cardinality fix (§3 Relationship 5 area / action item 8), and
the `programmes` vs. `coach_programmes` dead-column finding (§4, action item
9 — investigation continued, not yet resolved). Regenerate it (or at least
re-check the "At risk" rows) whenever a migration touches `status`,
`*_allowlist`, `*session_limits`, `peer_coaching_opt_in`, or
`user_module_access`.

Legend: ✅ in sync · ⚠️ at risk (works today, no guardrail against drift) · ❌ gap (one side has no mirror).

## 7. Triads (Phase 3): admin-assigned groups, self-service booking

Unlike every relationship in §3, triad membership is admin-curated (`triad_groups`, `20260903130000_*.sql`) but the *booking* itself has no counterpart-eligibility check to enforce — any of the 3 already-fixed members may schedule a session for their own group, and the roles on that session must themselves be drawn from the same 3 members.

| Rule | Enforced in DB | Enforced in frontend | In sync? |
